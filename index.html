Industrial Rigor

Data Cleansing & Integrity Audit

Automated validation rules applied to the Mendeley safety-boot distribution dataset

01

Duplicate Pick Verification

102,201 exact duplicate rows confirmed in Picking_Wave.csv. Validated that each row represents exactly 1 picked unit (qty=1). Kept to avoid undercounting pick workload.

02

Trolley Capacity Bounds

74 waves exceeded nominal trolley capacity (27 units, observed max 30). Flagged and handled with dual-trolley trip mechanics during route simulation.

03

Shoe Size Decimal Encoding

41,549 size entries encoded without decimal point (e.g. 85 for size 8.5). Normalized automatically by dividing values > 20 by 10.

04

Order-to-Picking Reconciliation

77 waves present in orders but missing from picking logs. Excluded cleanly from routing benchmarks to guarantee strict comparability.
